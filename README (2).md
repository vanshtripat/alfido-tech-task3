## 💡 3 Actionable Insights

**1. MENA Region Opportunity**  
Saudi Arabia drives 21% of traffic. Prioritize Arabic (RTL) landing pages and MENA-targeted campaigns to improve conversion in this high-intent region.

**2. Close the Preview → Click Gap**  
28,531 previews don't convert to clicks. A/B test richer preview cards (audio snippets, artist bios) to push preview-to-click conversion up by at least 5%.

**3. Mid-Week Content Scheduling**  
Traffic dips ~16% by Wednesday–Thursday. Scheduling promotional pushes on those days can flatten the weekly curve and smooth revenue distribution.
